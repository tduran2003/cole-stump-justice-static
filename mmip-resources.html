🤝 MMIP Resources

Learn about Missing and Murdered Indigenous Persons (MMIP) and organizations working to bring justice and awareness.

What is MMIP?

Missing and Murdered Indigenous Persons (MMIP) refers to Indigenous people, particularly women and girls, who have gone missing or been murdered. This crisis disproportionately affects Native American, Alaska Native, and Indigenous communities across North America.

The MMIP crisis is a result of systemic racism, inadequate law enforcement response, and historical trauma. Cole Stump's case is part of this larger pattern of injustice.

By learning about MMIP and supporting advocacy efforts, we work toward a future where Indigenous lives are valued and protected.

MMIP Statistics

The scale of the MMIP crisis is staggering:

  • 4 in 5 Native American women experience violence in their lifetime
  • 1.5x higher murder rate for Native Americans compared to national average
  • Thousands of cases remain unsolved and under-investigated
  • Systemic barriers prevent adequate law enforcement response
