About This Vigil

Cole Stump's life was taken unjustly on October 12, 2020. This digital vigil serves as a space for the community to come together, remember Cole, and demand accountability.

Every message, every voice, and every act of solidarity contributes to the larger movement for justice. Together, we honor Cole's memory and fight for systemic change.

How You Can Help

  • Share Your Story: Tell us how Cole's case has impacted you or your community.
  • Advocate: Contact elected officials and demand accountability and policy changes.
  • Educate: Share information about Missing and Murdered Indigenous Persons (MMIP).
  • Support: Attend community events and stand in solidarity with Cole's family.
  • Document: Help gather and preserve evidence and testimony related to the case.
