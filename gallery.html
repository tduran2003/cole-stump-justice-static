About These Photos

This gallery contains photos of Cole Stump and moments from our community advocacy efforts. Each image tells a story of remembrance and solidarity.

To contribute photos to this gallery, please contact us directly. We welcome submissions from community members, family, and supporters.

Photo Guidelines

  • Photos should be respectful and appropriate for a memorial gallery
  • Include photo credit and date if available
  • Photos may be edited for size and quality
  • All submissions are reviewed before being added

Submit a Photo

To submit photos for the gallery, please email them to: photos@justice4colestump.com
