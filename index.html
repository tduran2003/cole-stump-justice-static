Justice for Cole Stump

Cole was murdered by Billings Police on October 12, 2020, while committing no crime. We demand justice, accountability, and transparency.

The Case at a Glance

On October 12, 2020, Cole Stump, a 29-year-old Indigenous father of five from Rocky Boy Reservation, was shot and killed by Billings Police officers Justin Bickford and Ryland Nelson. Despite committing no crime, the shooting was deemed "justified" by an inquest jury. We refuse to accept this outcome and demand a full investigation into police conduct, evidence tampering, and systemic failures.

The Shooting

Cole was shot by Billings Police on October 12, 2020, in circumstances that remain disputed and under investigation.

Evidence Tampering

Dispatch records (CAD logs) were altered 4 years after the shooting to edit information about the fire department responders.

Taser Timeline

Computer records show the Taser was fired 5 minutes after the shooting ended, contradicting the official narrative.

Critical Inconsistencies & Investigative Questions

Our deep review of the documented evidence, public files, and physical scene records has revealed contradictions that the official narrative leaves completely unaddressed:

  • • Missing & Mismarked Evidence: Out of 11 officer shell casings fired at the scene, only 10 were officially sent in for processing. Separate shell casings were located in completely different physical locations by different officers, yet were assigned the exact same evidence marker.
  • • Delayed Taser Deployment: Records indicate a severe timeline gap: a taser was deployed an inexplicable five minutes after the shooting had already occurred.
  • • Altered CAD Logs: Official Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) logs have not remained consistent. The CAD logs initially provided via public records requests in 2022 show clear, distinct alterations when compared directly against updated CAD logs requested and received in 2024.
  • • Unexplained Scene Activity: Witness accounts and scene monitoring identify two people walking directly toward the shooting scene right before it happened and walking away immediately after. Furthermore, an individual was observed completely changing clothes right next to a police unit within the active, cordoned scene area.

These facts do not align with a transparent investigation. We compile this data to ensure the details cannot be hidden, forgotten, or erased.
