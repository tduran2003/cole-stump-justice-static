Justice for Cole Stump

Cole Stump was killed by Billings Police Department officers on October 12, 2020—a day observed as both Native American Day and Columbus Day. Despite committing no crime, Cole's life was taken.

This portal serves as a digital hub for justice, accountability, and transparency. We believe in the power of community, evidence, and persistent advocacy. Every document, every timeline entry, and every message of support brings us closer to justice.

This is not just about Cole Stump. This is about all Missing and Murdered Indigenous Persons (MMIP) and the systemic failures that allow injustice to persist.

⚠️ Critical Unanswered Questions & Inconsistencies

The official narrative surrounding the events of October 12, 2020, leaves massive contradictions completely unaddressed. Our review of the documented evidence, public records, and physical scene details reveals discrepancies that demand transparent investigation:

  • Missing & Mismarked Shell Casings Out of 11 officer shell casings fired at the scene, only 10 were officially sent in for processing. Furthermore, separate shell casings were located in completely different physical locations by different responding officers, yet they were assigned the exact same evidence marker.
  • Delayed Taser Deployment Records indicate a severe gap in the tactical sequence: a taser was deployed an astronomical five minutes *after* the shooting had already occurred, raising immense questions about the timeline of force used.
  • Altered CAD Dispatch Logs Official Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) logs have not remained consistent. The CAD logs initially provided via records requests in 2022 show clear changes and alterations when compared directly against the updated CAD logs requested and received in 2024.
  • Unidentified Scene Activity & Wardrobe Changes Witness accounts and scene monitoring show two unidentified individuals actively walking directly toward the shooting scene right before it occurred, and then walking away immediately afterward. Additionally, an individual was observed completely changing clothes directly next to a parked police unit amidst the active scene.

These facts do not align with a transparent investigation. We compile this data to ensure the details cannot be hidden, forgotten, or erased.
