About This Case

Cole Stump was killed by Billings Police Department officers on October 12, 2020. Despite committing no crime, Cole's life was taken. This portal serves as a digital hub for justice, accountability, and transparency.

We believe in the power of community, evidence, and persistent advocacy. Every document, every timeline entry, and every message of support brings us closer to justice.

This is not just about Cole Stump. This is about all Missing and Murdered Indigenous Persons (MMIP) and the systemic failures that allow injustice to persist.
