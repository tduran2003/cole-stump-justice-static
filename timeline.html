Case Timeline

Key events and milestones in the Cole Stump case from October 12, 2020 to present.

October 12, 2020
Cole Stump Killed

Cole Stump is killed by Billings Police Department officers while committing no crime. The incident occurs without justification or legal basis.

October 13-31, 2020
Initial Investigation

Billings Police Department begins internal investigation. Community begins organizing for justice and accountability.

November 2020
First Public Records Request

Community members file initial Public Records Requests (PRR) demanding access to case documents, body camera footage, and investigation records.

December 2020 - June 2021
Multiple PRR Responses

Billings Police Department provides partial responses to PRRs. Community continues to demand complete transparency and accountability.

July 2021
Advocacy Intensifies

Community advocacy efforts expand. Witness statements are collected and documented. Legal analysis of the case begins.

August 2021 - December 2022
Ongoing Investigation & Documentation

Continued PRRs filed. Additional witness statements gathered. Timeline of events documented. Community maintains pressure for justice.

January 2023
Inquest Proceedings Begin

Official inquest into Cole Stump's death begins. Testimony is heard from witnesses, officers, and investigators.

February - June 2023
Inquest Testimony

Multiple witnesses testify. Evidence is presented. Community attends proceedings and documents testimony.

July 2023
Inquest Conclusion

Inquest concludes. Findings and recommendations are issued. Community continues advocacy for implementation of recommendations.

August 2023 - Present
Ongoing Advocacy

Community continues to demand accountability, policy changes, and justice. This digital portal is created to centralize evidence and advocacy efforts.

2024 - Present
Continued Justice Efforts

Community maintains pressure through advocacy, documentation, and public awareness. All evidence is preserved and made publicly available.
